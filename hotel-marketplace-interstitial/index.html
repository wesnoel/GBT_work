Egencia project

Hotel marketplace value interstitial

Four directions, plus three variations on D, for the variant of the SRP loading modal. After a user clicks search, the SRP takes about 6 to 10 seconds to load. This is the moment to reinforce the breadth of the Egencia marketplace without making the wait feel longer.

Source: PRD-EGE-20261013 and the design brief Fidelity: Low-fi, high interaction Focus: Desktop. Mobile web likely keeps the current modal Footprint: 453 x 568, same as control Status: Draft for review

How to review. Every page has the same controls: results-ready time (2s to 10s), a minimum display time, desktop or mobile (mobile keeps the current modal by default), first-time or repeat exposure, reduce motion, a control comparison, and a simulated variant failure. Settings carry across pages so you can compare directions under the same conditions.
  • Claim wording: "True for everyone" holds for every user and needs no personalization. "PRD claims" uses the stronger wording (best prices, your negotiated rates) and needs substantiation first.
  • Wait time: a minimum display time longer than the results time shows in red, because the brief says the variant must not outlast the control.
  • Accessibility: the panel logs what a screen reader would announce, including where focus lands when the modal closes.
