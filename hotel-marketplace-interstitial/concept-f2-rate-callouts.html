F2. Rate callouts (animated)

The hotel stays put and sends signals outward. Each callout is drawn from the hotel with a leader line, a ripple spreads from the building and the hotel gives a small beat as the card slides in. Hover a callout to emphasize its line.

Motion: ripple, leader draw, card slide, hotel beat. Four callouts is a lot for 6 to 10 seconds.
