Hotel Modify Hub

Three concepts for the landing page of hotel Modify. The Hub is where a Traveler, Travel Arranger or GBT Counselor chooses what to change on an existing booking: guest details (Name, Special request, Loyalty program), Dates, or Room. Only the landing page is designed here. Clicking an option shows where the next flow would start.

Source: PRD-EGE-20261117-3 and its UX spec Fidelity: Low-fi, medium interactive Status: Draft for review

Selected direction: Concept A

A — Flow 1 of 3

Soft changes

Opens from the Guest details card. Name, Special request and Loyalty program on one page with per-section validation, a retry-in-place failure banner and a discard dialog. Flows 2 (Date) and 3 (Room) are not built yet.

Landing page concepts

A — Cards

Three cards

Booking summary on top, then one card per flow. Guest details lists Name, Special request and Loyalty as direct rows, so common edits are one click.

Open wireframe → B — Editable list

Booking details with Edit

The booking itself is the Hub. Each detail has an Edit link, grouped by price impact, with a sticky summary alongside. Fastest for people who know what they want.

What do you want to change?

Pick an intent, see what is involved in a preview panel, then continue. Slower by one click, clearest about price impact before committing.

How to review

Use the dark review bar on every concept to switch the viewer (Traveler, Travel arranger, GBT counselor) and the booking state (all changes available, Date change not available, booking cannot be modified). Those settings carry across concepts. Decisions and open questions are in design-decisions.md.
