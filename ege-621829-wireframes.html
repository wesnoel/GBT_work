Copy exploration · S1 — Timing known, amount unknown

Alternatives to "Amount unknown"

A few things to keep in mind before choosing: for S1 specifically, the penalty does apply — the traveler is past the deadline. So the phrasing shouldn't imply uncertainty about whether it applies, only about the specific figure. The reason we don't have a number is that Amadeus didn't provide it as structured data — the framing should quietly put that authority at the hotel, not at Egencia.

Action-oriented — hotel is the source of truth
  • Contact hotel to confirm
  • Confirm with hotel
  • Hotel to advise
  • Hotel will confirm amount
  • Contact hotel for details
Process / timing — forward-looking, not a gap
  • Pending hotel confirmation
  • Confirmed upon cancellation
  • Confirmed at time of cancellation
  • Awaiting hotel confirmation
Policy reference — authoritative, deflects to source
  • Refer to hotel policy
  • Per hotel cancellation policy
  • See hotel cancellation terms
  • Subject to hotel terms
Neutral / soft — minimal, no blame
  • To be confirmed
  • Not yet confirmed
  • Check hotel policy
→
Design instinct

Contact hotel to confirm or Pending hotel confirmation work best for S1. Both subtly communicate that the hotel is the authoritative source — not that Egencia is missing data. "Pending hotel confirmation" has a slightly more professional, process-oriented tone that travel industry users would recognize. "Contact hotel to confirm" is more direct and gives the traveler a clear next action.

Whatever lands here will also affect the email and Change HUB copy once those surfaces are scoped in, so voice/L10n review matters even more for the final pick.
