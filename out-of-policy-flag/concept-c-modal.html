Decisions applied here

Small/medium/large change only the flag icon's size (16/18/20px) — every trigger on this page (standalone and in-context) opens the exact same modal, since a full takeover's content doesn't need to vary just because the icon that opened it was small or large · click/tap only, no hover-open — a full-screen takeover should never appear just from a mouse passing over the flag or a keyboard focus landing on it · no idle auto-dismiss — explicit close (×), backdrop click, or Escape only, and focus returns to the trigger that opened it on close · the hotel card example defaults to the small icon size, shown with every optional data slot filled in (reviews + colleague reviews, sustainability certified, coworker bookings, perk strip) to match the real card's maximum-density state.
