Concept C — Modal

A full dialog takeover for maximum clarity and emphasis. Click or tap only — a modal should never appear from hover or keyboard focus alone. No idle auto-dismiss either: once it takes over the screen, it stays open until the traveler explicitly closes it, clicks the backdrop, or presses Escape.

Decisions applied here

Click/tap only, every trigger on this page (standalone and in-context) opens the same shared modal, sized to match whichever flag was clicked · no idle auto-dismiss — explicit close (×), backdrop click, or Escape only, and focus returns to the trigger that opened it on close · small stays to a single "Got it" acknowledgment; large adds a secondary "Request exception" action alongside the policy link, since a full takeover has the room to offer a real next step.
