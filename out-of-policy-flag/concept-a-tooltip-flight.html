Decisions applied here

Same tooltip component, interaction, and five placement variants as the Hotel version — see Concept A for that write-up · what's different is the host card: the real Air SRP flight card (references/pattern-flight-srp.md) compares three fare tiers per row (Essential/Smart/Elite), and the out-of-policy flag lives specifically on the Elite column's price, not a single price block like Hotel · within that price, the flag icon comes after the price value — the reverse order from Hotel, confirmed by design review · the flag still sits near the card's right edge, so the same below-left/above-left placement variants apply for the same reason · content is Flight's real list-format data (two numbered violation reasons: the reference-price ceiling and the cabin-class rule) instead of Hotel's single sentence. Full write-up: design-decisions.md.
