Concept B — Popover dialog

A heavier, structured popover — still anchored to the flag, not a full modal. Has a real header (icon + title) and an explicit close button. Opens on hover, click, or tap — the standard interaction for both tooltip and popover going forward. Small/medium/large only change the flag icon's size (16/18/20px) — the popover's content, width, padding, and type are the same at every size.

Decisions applied here

Small/medium/large change only the flag icon's size (16/18/20px) — the popover itself (width, header, body, footer, type) is identical at every size, shown standalone above · hover, click, and tap all open it — the standard interaction for both tooltip and popover going forward, superseding an earlier click/tap-only version of this concept · has an explicit close (×) button, but still auto-dismisses 3s after the pointer leaves in case someone opens it and walks away without closing · keyboard focus reveals it via :focus-visible, and every close path (timeout, × button, outside click, Escape) calls blur() · five placement variants (below, above, left, below-left, above-left) — below/above center the popover on the trigger, left is vertically centered beside it, and the "-left" variants stay flush to the trigger's right edge and grow leftward instead of rightward, which is what the hotel card actually needs since its flag sits at the right edge of the price column · the hotel card examples default to the small icon size. Full write-up: design-decisions.md.
