Concept B — Popover dialog — Flight

Same popover component as the Hotel version (see Concept B), applied to the real Air SRP flight card instead of the Hotel SRP card. The flag sits on the Elite fare column's price, one of three fare tiers compared side by side — a genuinely different placement context than Hotel's single price block, and the flag comes after the price here, not before it like Hotel.

Decisions applied here

Same popover component, interaction, and five placement variants as the Hotel version — see Concept B for that write-up · what's different is the host card: the real Air SRP flight card (references/pattern-flight-srp.md) compares three fare tiers per row (Essential/Smart/Elite), and the out-of-policy flag lives specifically on the Elite column's price, not a single price block like Hotel · within that price, the flag icon comes after the price value — the reverse order from Hotel, confirmed by design review · the flag still sits near the card's right edge, so the same below-left/above-left placement variants apply for the same reason · content is Flight's real list-format data (two numbered violation reasons: the reference-price ceiling and the cabin-class rule) instead of Hotel's single sentence. Full write-up: design-decisions.md.
