Decisions applied here

Badge shows only when the feed flags a hotel certified — Park Central and The Manhattan Club show no badge (fail closed) · hovering/focusing the badge previews the certification name; clicking it always opens the full list · the "Sustainability certified" chip here actually filters the list, standing in for its real placement nested under Amenities/Property in the filter panel · zero-result state reuses the standard empty-state pattern with a one-click clear.
Multi-certification hotels: citizenM New York Times Square carries three certifications — the tooltip shows the primary one plus a "+2 more" hint, and clicking opens the side sheet with the full list.
