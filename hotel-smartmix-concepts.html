3 Design Concepts
Three approaches to surfacing Egencia Preferred, Company Preferred, and Recommended results with progressive loading. Each concept is interactive with 3 loading states.
01
Concept 01
Three-Column Grid
Egencia Preferred, Company Preferred, and Recommended side by side. Columns load independently — Expedia arrives first, Amadeus and SmartMix follow.
Parallel loading Equal weight Scannable
02
Concept 02
Tabbed Sources
A tab bar switches between Egencia Preferred, Company Preferred, and Recommended. Expedia tab populates first; other tabs unlock as their data arrives.
Focused view Source filter Low noise
03
Concept 03
Ranked Feed
A single unified list ranked by SmartMix score. Preferred and Negotiated hotels surface first inline. Results stream in as each source loads — no layout shift.
Single list SmartMix ranked Streaming load
