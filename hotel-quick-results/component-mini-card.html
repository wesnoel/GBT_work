Hotel Mini Card — states & variance
291px wide, per the "Hotel Mini Card" frames in your Figma file. One component, many optional slots — most real cards will land somewhere between "full data" and "minimal data," not at either extreme. Click any loaded card to simulate the PDP click-through; hover/click the flag icon on the price line to see the out-of-policy popover.
Content rules (per your latest guidance): price + the "$X w taxes and fees" line always show, and the price block always sits flush at the bottom of the card regardless of what's above it. A Reviews section always shows too — the public score line is required, the Colleague Reviews line is optional. Stars (3–4★ range for these examples) and distance always show. Everything else — badges, sustainability cert, coworker %, perk strip — may or may not appear.
