# Context Keeper — TL;DR

**The problem:** Somiya is juggling a lot — Slack questions, ticket switches, discovery conversations — and wants to stay more on top of where everything stands. Right now, keeping track means piecing it back together manually across notes, Slack history, and memory. It works, but it takes more effort than it should.

**The idea:** A lightweight personal workspace that tracks her place for her. Every task gets a "context card" — where things stand, open questions, next steps. Jump away, come back, and she's re-oriented in seconds instead of cross-referencing three different tools.

**The bonus:** Every card she marks done becomes a quiet accomplishment log. By review time, she already has a running record of wins and manager praise, without ever sitting down to write it.

**Why it works:** It only counts if it's fast. The bet isn't "give her somewhere to take notes" — it's that if capturing context takes seconds, not minutes, it becomes a natural part of her workflow instead of one more thing to maintain.

**Scope right now:** Built for Somiya, by hand-entry, as a hackathon prototype today. Slack/Jira integrations, team rollout, and admin features are deliberately future phases, not part of this build.

**The line:** Stay on top of everything. Track your wins without trying.
