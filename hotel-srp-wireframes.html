Hotel SRP — Progressive Rendering & SmartMix

Five wireframe concepts for the new component above the existing hotel list · 1200px · Grayscale

5 Concepts 3 Interactive States Each Desktop Web · 1200px Matches current Egencia SRP layout
01

Horizontal Strip

Compact scrollable row of cards above the list/map. Up to 5 per provider, resolves from skeletons in place.

02

Grid Panel

Dark-header panel with 5-column card grid. Ranking rationale always visible in the header.

03

Grouped List

Results blend into the top of the list column with source-group labels. No separate section panel.

04

Feature + Cards

Large featured card for top SmartMix pick with full reasoning, plus 4 compact companion cards.

05

Tabbed Sources

Tab bar: All Results / Expedia / Preferred & Negotiated. Source filtering at section level.

C1 — Horizontal Strip 1 of 5 State

Horizontal Strip

A compact, horizontally-scrollable band of cards sits between the filter bar and the existing list/map. Cards resolve from skeletons as each rate source loads. Preferred and Negotiated results carry a badge; SmartMix badge appears once ranking data is available.

Low vertical footprint Horizontal scroll for overflow In-place skeleton resolution Up to 5 per provider
